About Me

Dedicated to Empathy, Precision & Healing

Hello! I am Dr. Nipuni Lelwala, a medical graduate from the prestigious Manipal College of Medical Sciences (MCOMS) in Pokhara, Nepal. My medical education provided me with rigorous clinical exposure across diverse patient demographics, fostering strong diagnostic acumen and adaptability in high-pressure healthcare environments.

I believe that effective medical practice bridges scientific rigor with genuine human connection. Whether managing acute cases in hospital wards or counseling patients on preventive health, my priority is ensuring every individual feels heard, respected, and actively involved in their care journey.

Holistic Approach

Treating the whole person rather than just isolated symptoms, integrating physical and mental well-being.

Evidence-Based Practice

Staying up to date with current clinical guidelines and medical research to deliver safe, effective care.
